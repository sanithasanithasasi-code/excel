# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data to identify sales trends, product performance, profitability, customer behavior, and regional performance.

The analysis helps understand business performance and provides useful insights for better decision-making.

## 🎯 Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and profit.
- Identify top-performing product categories.
- Compare sales across different regions.
- Analyze customer ratings and payment methods.
- Understand discounts and delivery performance.
- Identify business trends and patterns.

## 📂 Dataset Information
- **Total Records:** 5,000
- **Total Columns:** 17
- **Unique Orders:** 5,000
- **Unique Customers:** 989
- **Date Range:** 2022–2035

### Key Columns
- Order ID
- Order Date
- Customer ID
- Product Category
- Region
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Cost Price
- Discount
- Payment Method
- Delivery Days
- Customer Rating
- Profit
- Revenue

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Pivot Tables
- Charts and Visualizations
- Data Analysis
- Data Cleaning

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Quantity Sold
- Average Customer Rating
- Average Delivery Days

## 🔍 Key Analysis
1. **Sales Analysis:** Examined overall revenue and sales performance.
2. **Profit Analysis:** Evaluated profitability across product categories.
3. **Category Analysis:** Compared Electronics, Clothing, Home, and Beauty.
4. **Regional Analysis:** Analyzed sales performance across North, South, East, and West.
5. **Customer Analysis:** Examined customer ratings and purchasing behavior.
6. **Payment Analysis:** Compared different payment methods.

## 📈 Key Findings
- Electronics generated the highest revenue among all product categories.
- Clothing was the second-highest revenue-generating category.
- The West region recorded the highest revenue.
- The dataset contains 5,000 orders from 989 unique customers.
- Customer ratings and delivery times provide additional insights into customer experience.

## 💡 Conclusion
This project helped analyze e-commerce sales performance using Microsoft Excel. Through data analysis, Pivot Tables, and visualizations, important insights were identified about revenue, profit, product categories, regional performance, and customer behavior.

Overall, the project improved my practical understanding of data analysis and business decision-making.
